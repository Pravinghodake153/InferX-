ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR BIDDERS

The following criteria must be fulfilled by all bidders:

1. The bidder must have a minimum annual turnover of ₹5 crore for each of the last 3 financial years.
2. The bidder must possess a valid ISO 9001:2015 certification.
3. The bidder must have successfully completed at least 3 similar solar panel installation projects in the last 5 years.
4. The bidder must be registered under GST and provide a valid GSTIN.
5. The bidder must have a minimum net worth of ₹2 crore as on 31st March 2024.

[Page 3]
GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS

All bidders must submit the following documents along with their proposal:
- Audited Balance Sheet for FY 2021-22, 2022-23, and 2023-24
- Copy of ISO certification
- Completion certificates of past projects
- GST registration certificate
- CA-certified net worth certificate

The evaluation will be conducted in two stages: Technical evaluation followed by Financial evaluation.
Only bidders meeting all mandatory eligibility criteria will be considered for financial evaluation.
