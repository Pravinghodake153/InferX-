ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR BIDDERS

1. The bidder must have a minimum annual turnover of ₹10 crore for each of the last 3 financial years.
2. The bidder must possess a valid ISO 27001 (Information Security) certification.
3. The bidder must have completed at least 5 similar IT infrastructure projects for government organizations in the last 5 years.
4. The bidder must be registered under GST and provide a valid GSTIN.
5. The bidder must have a minimum net worth of ₹4 crore as on 31st March 2024.
6. The bidder must not be blacklisted by any Central/State Government department.

[Page 3]
SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

- Audited financial statements for last 3 years
- ISO 27001 certificate (valid)
- Project completion certificates
- Self-declaration regarding blacklisting
- GST registration certificate
- Net worth certificate from Chartered Accountant
